# Saheli design spec (DESIGN.md)
Follow this exactly for every page. When it disagrees with anything else, this file wins. Desktop sizes are measured at 1440px wide; phones at 390px. Screenshots in design-reference/ (desktop-NN-name.png and mobile-NN-name.png) show every page: match them.

## 1. Tokens
Body: background var(--bg), text var(--ink), Figtree 16px, line-height 1.6, colour transitions 0.6s. Headings use text-wrap: balance. Focus ring: 2px solid var(--primary-ink), offset 2px, radius 6px. Selection: surface-2 background.
- `.font-display`: DM Serif Display, weight 400, letter-spacing -0.005em. Used for page titles, card titles, big numbers.
- `.label` (eyebrows): 0.72rem, bold 700, letter-spacing 0.09em, UPPERCASE, colour var(--accent-ink) (sage green #5E8A71 in the default theme).
- `.tabular`: tabular-nums for every number.
- Radius: cards 20px (--radius-card: 1.25rem); hero cards (Today's cycle card, pregnancy card, stage card) 32px; chips and buttons fully round; small tiles 16px.
- Shadow --shadow-soft: 0 1px 2px rgb(58 30 42 / .04), 0 8px 24px -12px rgb(58 30 42 / .12) (primary buttons, phone mockup).
- Card: white surface, 1px border var(--line), radius 20px, padding 20px (24px from sm). Gap between stacked cards 20px; two-column grids use 20px gaps.
- Tinted panel: background var(--surface-2), no border, radius 20px (e.g. "What helps in your luteal phase").
- Alert card: 2px border var(--primary) for "Call your doctor" lists; red alert (pain diary flag): background #FDE2E2, 2px border #9B1B30 at 50%, text #5c1020.
- Highlighted card: 2px border primary at 40% (the 10-second check-in, Upload a report at 50%), "From your reports" 1px primary at 60%.

Themes: the root has data-theme, data-mode (light | dark) and in the app data-mood. Every colour is a CSS variable: --bg --surface --surface-2 --line --ink --ink-soft --primary --primary-ink --on-primary --accent --accent-ink --on-accent, plus phase colours --ph-period --ph-follicular --ph-ovulation --ph-luteal.

## 2. App shell
Desktop (768px and up): a 2-column grid, 248px sidebar + content. The sidebar is sticky, full height, white, with a 1px right border, 16px side and 24px top padding: the logo (md, with tagline) at top; then the 11 menu links (40px rows, 12px radius, icon + label 15px; the active one has a surface-2 background, primary-ink text and semibold); then at the bottom: the "Get help" pill (full width, 40px, blush surface-2 background, 1px primary/40 border, primary-ink text, HandHeart icon; fills primary with white text on hover), the profile switcher (white card with a round initial avatar in primary, name and "Switch profile", chevron), and a "Private by design" note (surface-2 panel, radius 16px, shield icon in accent-ink: "Your health data stays on this device.").
Content: 40px padding on desktop (24px tablet, 16px phone), 112px bottom padding on phones for the bottom bar, inner column max-width 1024px, centred.
Phone (under 768px): a sticky 64px top bar (bg at 90% with blur, bottom border): logo (sm) left; right side the "Get help" pill (32px tall, 12px text), a 32px round initial avatar (profile switcher) and a gear icon to Settings. A fixed bottom bar (white 95% with blur, top border, safe-area padding), 5 equal items with icon over label: Today, Cycle, Nourish, Bestie (her companion name), More. The active item is primary-ink.
Grouped pages show tabs above the page header: pill tabs (36px tall, 16px padding, semibold); the active tab is filled ink (#3A1E2A) with white text, others surface-2. On phones the tab row scrolls sideways, fades at the right edge (mask 85%) and scrolls the open tab to the centre.
Sample banner (when a sample is open): full-width dark ink card (#3A1E2A), radius 16px, 56px tall, sparkles icon, "Sample data: Priya, regular cycle", right side "Switch sample" (dark translucent pill) and "Exit sample" (white pill). It sits above the tabs on every app page.
PageHeader: eyebrow (.label), title in DM Serif 2.1rem (2.6rem from sm), lead in ink-soft max 672px wide, 32px margin below. Today uses its own header: the date in ink-soft 14px, "Hello, Priya" in DM Serif ~2.6rem, and on the right a phase pill (surface-2 background, a coloured dot in the phase colour, phase name).
